How does the Security Score work?

Every project starts at 100. Points are deducted for each finding based on its severity. Each severity level has a cap so a single category cannot tank your entire score.

CRITICAL -25 per finding max -75
HIGH -10 per finding max -40
MEDIUM -5 per finding max -25
LOW -2 per finding max -10
90–100 Ready to deploy  •  70–89 Fix HIGH items first  •  40–69 Do not deploy  •  0–39 Stop and fix
